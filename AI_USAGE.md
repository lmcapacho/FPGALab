# AI-assisted development

FPGALab is a human-directed open-source project initiated and maintained by
Luis Miguel Capacho.

Generative AI coding tools have been used to assist with implementation,
refactoring, testing, and documentation. Product requirements, architectural
decisions, integration, review, validation, maintenance, and release decisions
are performed by the project maintainer and human contributors.

AI systems are development tools. They are not listed as authors, contributors,
maintainers, or copyright holders. Every accepted change remains subject to
human review and the project's tests and release process.
